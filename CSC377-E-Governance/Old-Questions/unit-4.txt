Security Management Model: FIG
- generic description of what org should do to provide secure environment for itself

1. User Level: regulates who enters system and what actions they can perform

- identity management:
	- issues unique digital credentials liek ussernme and paswords to legal users to establish verified digital identities

- access management:
	- match presented credential against registries to authenticates users
		- level 0: basic registration with username password
		- level 1: username password combined wuth OTP token and UID card number
		- level 2: hardware/software token combined with PIN
		- level 3: biometric authentication like fingerprint, iris scan

- interaction management:
	- integrity, confidentialiy, non repudation using PKI, gigtal signatures, hardware tokens and asymmtetric key crypto

2. Transport Level: protects data in transit across internal and public internet channels

- secure communication system:
	- VPN and isolated network channels to secure data transfer between governemtn agencies and remote users

- cryptographic system:
	- encryption protocls like SSL TLS to encrypt data payloads, preveting eavesdropping and packet sniffing

3. ICT Assets Level: protect central servers, hardware, databases, network equipment using physical and electronic defenses

- physical security: 
	- secures physical data centers against unauthorized physical entry and envirometnal hazards through biometric acesss 	controls, dust prrof enclosures, CCTV surveliiance

- electronic security:
	- proitets against digital threats
		- anti virus system: defends servers agsinst virurs, worms and trojon horse that corrupt storage, disrupt 		operations
		- enterprise friewalls: harwadre/software security gateways that separate secure private intranets from 		untrusted public networks
